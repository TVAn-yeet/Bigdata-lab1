Task 1 establishes a bounded tenant using ResourceQuota team-budget. The namespace is limited to 2 CPU requested, 4 CPU limited, 2 GiB requested memory, 4 GiB limited memory, 8 GiB requested storage, two PVCs, and ten Pods. A small client Pod requesting 100m CPU and 128Mi memory was admitted successfully. A second Pod requesting 3 CPU was rejected because its CPU request exceeded the namespace quota. No quota increase was required.
